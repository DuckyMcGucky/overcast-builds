Alpha 0.1 (build 1) - 2026-10-11

  • Redesigned Settings and Store: sky header, cards with colored icons, and course cards with art and prices. Settings now opens full-screen.
  • New top-bar counter for mock orals, radio, and Explains. It turns orange when you're running low, and tapping it opens the Prep Pass.
  • Smoother page transitions (slide and fade).
  • Purchases are mocked in this build, so nothing is really charged.
  • Extra courses are $4.99 each. The Checkride Prep Pass is $19.99, can be bought again, and adds 5 mock orals, 10 radio sessions, and 50 Explains, with no expiry.
  • Only Private Pilot (PPL) is released. Other courses say "Coming soon" and can't be bought, and first-launch setup offers PPL only.
  • Radio ATC: if the scripted reply doesn't fit, an AI fallback (Grok 4.5, low effort) answers, with a 6-second timeout and "Standby" while it thinks.
  • Knowledge web reveal animation polish.
  • One quiz per line item, with retry.
  • Scripts synced with Google Docs: Area I keeps your and Tim's edits, Areas II-XII were refreshed from the app, and there's a new Area X doc. Cut-off titles in the docs were fixed.
  • "HC" (human certified) stamp list started, so we can track which scripts are verified.
  • About card in Settings now shows the version, e.g. "Alpha 0.1 (1)".
